Signed imbalance memory

Buy-minus-sell ACF: .173 at 1 s, .049 at 5 s, .019 at 10 s, .011 at 30 s, .0094 at 1 min, .0025 at 5 min, and .0020 at 1 h. Directional memory is therefore dramatically shorter/weaker than total-activity memory.

Interpretation

The raw activity covariance is not itself the latent C(h): it contains Hawkes offspring plus latent/background variation. It nevertheless rules out using a single OU as a faithful model of total observed persistence. A low-dimensional exponential mixture is the natural Markov approximation; a tempered power-law is a compact parametric alternative. Separating Hawkes phi_ij from latent C requires joint/nonparametric Hawkes estimation followed by residual covariance analysis.
