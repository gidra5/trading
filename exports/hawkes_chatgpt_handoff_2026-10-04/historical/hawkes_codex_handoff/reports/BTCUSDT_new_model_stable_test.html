BTCUSDT stable multiscale Hawkes + latent background

Data: 14,464,973 BTCUSDT spot aggregate trades, reduced to causal 1-second buy/sell counts. Chronological split: 60% train, 20% validation region, 20% test. Forecast origins are 3,000 identical points from the held-out test region, each with 1h future available.

Model

Observed Hawkes traces use normalized exponentials at τ={1,3,10,30,100,300}s. Nonnegative excitation coefficients are fit on training data. The integrated branching matrix is rescaled to spectral radius 0.92, ensuring stable open-loop recursion. The latent background is an additive activity/imbalance state represented by OU/exponential modes τ={17,249,5151,50000}s. Stationary mode variances are fit to residual training covariance; states are updated causally with a Kalman-style filter.

Important: this is a clean causal/stability ablation of the new architecture, but not yet the final joint maximum-likelihood fit. Hawkes coefficients and latent covariance were estimated in two stages (nonnegative regression + residual covariance fit). The next optimization should jointly maximize predictive count likelihood.

Fitted structure

Hawkes branching spectral radius: 0.92. Fitted residual latent stationary variances (activity modes): [2.747, 0, 0, 0.150]; imbalance modes: [1.184, 0, 0.061, 0.189]. Thus after the Hawkes component is removed, this fit wants a strong fast ~17s latent component plus a very slow component; the intermediate activity modes collapse to zero. This is evidence against blindly retaining every hand-picked scale.

Interpretation

The latent state mainly improves calibration rather than ranking/regime correlation: correlations are nearly unchanged while MAE and R² improve at medium/long horizons. This is what we would expect if frozen Hawkes already tracks whether activity is high/low, while the filtered background corrects the persistent level. Direction remains substantially harder; the latent imbalance component helps through 15m but does not improve 1h imbalance correlation in this fit.

Validation caveats / next fit

To call this the final model, jointly fit the Hawkes excitation, latent process variances/timescales, observation model, and stability margin by chronological predictive likelihood. Then compare negative-binomial/Poisson observation noise, re-estimate confidence intervals with non-overlapping block bootstrap, and evaluate event-level short-horizon likelihood alongside count forecasts. The present result establishes that the stable latent-background architecture is useful and numerically well behaved; it does not establish that the current parameter estimates are optimal.
